### `> WHO AM I?`

I'm an **independent researcher** based in Pune, India, working at the intersection of **maths, computation and biology**.

My work centres on **identifiability**: the difference between what a model *can* explain and what the data can actually distinguish. It's an unglamorous question that sits underneath a lot of famous ones.

Backpropagation can't run in the brain — so which biologically plausible rule does the brain use, and could we even tell from the recordings we're able to make? Epigenetic clocks predict mortality — but are their CpG sites **drivers** of ageing, or **passengers** recording the damage?

I work with **public datasets**, on **ordinary hardware**, and I publish the code, the pre-registrations, and the analyses that didn't work alongside the ones that did.
